## Mission Briefing

ESG stands for Environmental, Social, and Governance.

- **Environmental:** carbon emissions, water use, deforestation, biodiversity.
- **Social:** labor rights, community impact, supply-chain ethics.
- **Governance:** board diversity, corruption risk, transparency.

Companies are rated by agencies (MSCI, Sustainalytics) and investors use those scores to allocate capital. Learn the vocabulary before touching any cloud service.
